🥩 GEOFF'S TOP PICKS — Steakhouses & NYC Institutions

Keens Steakhouse #1 GEOFF PICK NYC INSTITUTION STEAKHOUSE
$$$$ | 72 W 36th St (Garment District) | 0.9 mi from theater
Established 1885. This is THE classic New York steakhouse. Famous for the 90,000 clay pipes hanging from the ceiling (including pipes belonging to Teddy Roosevelt, JFK, and Babe Ruth). The mutton chop is legendary — it's their signature. Old-school, dark wood, historic, unapologetically masculine. This is where you go for a quintessential NYC steakhouse experience.

Perfect for: Pre-theater dinner (5:00 PM reservation recommended)
Must-order: Mutton chop, dry-aged steaks, scotch selection
🌐 Website | 📅 Reserve on OpenTable
Gallaghers Steakhouse CLOSEST STEAKHOUSE NYC CLASSIC STEAKHOUSE
$$$$ | 228 West 52nd Street | 0.02 mi from theater
Since 1927. Right next door to the theater — you literally can't get closer. The iconic neon sign is a NYC landmark. Dry-aged steaks in a window display, old-school atmosphere, red leather banquettes. This is where theater-goers have dined for nearly a century.

Perfect for: Convenient pre-theater dinner (5:30 PM)
Must-order: Dry-aged ribeye, classic martini
🌐 Website | 📅 Reserve on OpenTable
The Palm West Side NYC INSTITUTION STEAKHOUSE
$$$$ | 250 West 50th Street | 0.13 mi from theater
Legendary steakhouse known for steaks, lobsters, and caricatures of famous regulars on the walls. Classic power-dining spot since the 1920s. The Palm is a steakhouse institution with locations worldwide, but this is the original NYC vibe.

📅 Reserve on OpenTable

⭐ MICHELIN-STARRED ITALIAN

Marea ⭐ MICHELIN STAR ITALIAN FANCY NIGHT OUT
$$$$ | 240 Central Park South | 0.7 mi from theater
Michael White's flagship. Michelin-starred Italian on Central Park South. The room is stunning — Indonesian rosewood, airy, elegant. The focus is coastal Italian seafood: crudo, pasta, whole fish. This is where you go when you want a special occasion dinner with impeccable service and serious food cred. James Beard Award winner.

Perfect for: Fancy pre-theater dinner (5:00 PM)
Must-order: Fusilli with red wine braised octopus, crudo selection
🌐 Website | 📅 Reserve on OpenTable
Becco ITALIAN THEATER CLASSIC
$$ | 355 W 46th St (Restaurant Row) | 0.3 mi from theater
Lidia Bastianich's Theater District institution. Fixed-price pasta tasting menu ($29.95), unlimited wine included. This is the theater crowd's go-to for reliable, affordable Italian before a show. Not Michelin-starred but a beloved NYC institution.

Perfect for: Pre-theater prix fixe, wine lovers
📅 Reserve on OpenTable

Le Bernardin ⭐⭐⭐ 3 MICHELIN STARS ULTIMATE SPLURGE
