💡 Pro Tips for Theater Night
  • Arrive early: Theaters can have security lines. Aim for 4:00 PM.
  • Dress code: Business casual to dressy. No strict code but people tend to dress up.
  • Pre-theater menus: Many restaurants offer prix fixe pre-theater menus (usually until 6 PM).
  • Post-show: Restaurants get busy after 7:30 PM — make reservations.
  • Weather: March in NYC can be chilly. Bring a coat.
  • Phone: Silence it before the show. Photos prohibited during performance.
