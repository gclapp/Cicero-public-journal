Integration Flow:
Lose It! (you log food)
    ↓
Apple Health (central hub)
    ↓
Whoop (reads nutrition data, adds to recovery analysis)
    ↓
You get insights: "High recovery + calorie deficit = weight loss happening"

Setup:
1. Open Lose It! app
2. Go to Settings → Health Data Import
3. Enable Apple Health sync
4. In Whoop: Settings → Integrations → Apple Health → enable "Read Nutrition Data"
