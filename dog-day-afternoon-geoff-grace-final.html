🥩 GEOFF'S TOP PICKS — Steakhouses & NYC Institutions

Keens Steakhouse #1 GEOFF PICK NYC INSTITUTION STEAKHOUSE
$$$$ | 72 W 36th St (Garment District) | 0.9 mi from theater
Established 1885. This is THE classic New York steakhouse. Famous for the 90,000 clay pipes hanging from the ceiling (including pipes belonging to Teddy Roosevelt, JFK, and Babe Ruth). The mutton chop is legendary — it's their signature. Old-school, dark wood, historic, unapologetically masculine.

Getting there from Westin: Taxi/Uber recommended (10-15 min, $15-20)

Perfect for: Pre-theater dinner (5:00 PM reservation)
Must-order: Mutton chop, dry-aged steaks, scotch selection
🌐 Website | 📅 Reserve on OpenTable
Gallaghers Steakhouse CLOSEST STEAKHOUSE NYC CLASSIC STEAKHOUSE
$$$$ | 228 West 52nd Street | 0.02 mi from theater
Since 1927. Right next door to the theater — you literally can't get closer. The iconic neon sign is a NYC landmark. Dry-aged steaks in a window display, old-school atmosphere, red leather banquettes.

Getting there from Westin: Walk 12-15 min north OR taxi 5 min ($10-12)

Perfect for: Convenient pre-theater dinner (5:30 PM)
Must-order: Dry-aged ribeye, classic martini
🌐 Website | 📅 Reserve on OpenTable
The Palm West Side NYC INSTITUTION STEAKHOUSE
$$$$ | 250 West 50th Street | 0.13 mi from theater
Legendary steakhouse known for steaks, lobsters, and caricatures of famous regulars on the walls. Classic power-dining spot since the 1920s.

Getting there from Westin: Walk 10 min north OR taxi 3 min ($8-10)

📅 Reserve on OpenTable

🍝 ITALIAN OPTIONS (Grace-Friendly)

Becco ITALIAN THEATER CLASSIC
$$ | 355 W 46th St (Restaurant Row) | 0.3 mi from theater
Lidia Bastianich's Theater District institution. Fixed-price pasta tasting menu ($29.95), unlimited wine included. This is the theater crowd's go-to for reliable, affordable Italian before a show. No seafood required — plenty of meat and pasta options.

Getting there from Westin: Walk 8 min north to 46th St

Perfect for: Pre-theater prix fixe, wine lovers
Must-order: Sinfonia di pasta (unlimited pasta tasting), osso buco
🌐 Website | 📅 Reserve on OpenTable
Toloache MEXICAN
$$ | 251 West 50th Street | 0.12 mi from theater
Upscale Mexican with excellent margaritas and guacamole. Lively but not too loud. Great for a fun pre-show meal. Extensive meat and vegetarian options — easy to avoid seafood.

Getting there from Westin: Walk 10 min north OR taxi 3 min

Must-order: Tableside guacamole, margaritas, carne asada
🌐 Website | 📅 Reserve on OpenTable

🍸 CLASSIC NYC BARS

Bar Centrale THEATER INSIDER POST-SHOW
$$$ | 324 W 46th St | 0.4 mi from theater
Hidden speakeasy above Tony's di Napoli. Theater industry hangout — actors, producers, and critics here after shows. Excellent martinis.

Getting there from theater: Walk 5 min south to 46th St

Perfect for: Post-show drinks (10:30 PM)
Russian Samovar & Tolstoy's Lounge NYC INSTITUTION
$$$ | 256 W 52nd Street | 0.06 mi from theater
Authentic Russian restaurant with vodka infusions (40+ house-infused vodkas), caviar, and live piano. Old-world charm, open late. Mikhail Baryshnikov is a regular.

Getting there from theater: Walk 1 min west
