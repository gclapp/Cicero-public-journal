Keens Steakhouse

Steakhouse NYC Institution $$$$
📍 Address: 72 W 36th St (Garment District)
📏 Distance: 0.9 mi from August Wilson Theatre
⭐ Rating: NYC Institution since 1885
The quintessential New York steakhouse. Famous for 90,000 clay pipes hanging from the ceiling (including Teddy Roosevelt, JFK, and Babe Ruth's pipes). Old-school, dark wood, historic atmosphere. This is where you go for the authentic NYC steakhouse experience.
🍽️ Must Order: The legendary mutton chop (their signature), dry-aged steaks, scotch selection
🌐 Website 📅 Reserve

Gallaghers Steakhouse

Steakhouse NYC Classic $$$$
📍 Address: 228 West 52nd Street
📏 Distance: 0.02 mi from August Wilson Theatre
⭐ Rating: Since 1927
Right next door to the theater — you literally can't get closer. The iconic neon sign is a NYC landmark. Dry-aged steaks in a window display, old-school atmosphere, red leather banquettes. Where theater-goers have dined for nearly a century.
🍽️ Must Order: Dry-aged ribeye, classic martini
🌐 Website 📅 Reserve

The Palm West Side

Steakhouse NYC Institution $$$$
📍 Address: 250 West 50th Street
📏 Distance: 0.13 mi from August Wilson Theatre
⭐ Rating: Since the 1920s
Legendary steakhouse known for steaks, lobsters, and caricatures of famous regulars on the walls. Classic power-dining spot. The Palm is a steakhouse institution with locations worldwide, but this is the original NYC vibe.
🍽️ Must Order: Nova Scotia lobster, dry-aged steaks
🌐 Website 📅 Reserve

Empire Steak House West

Steakhouse $$$$
📍 Address: 237 West 54th Street
📏 Distance: 0.11 mi from August Wilson Theatre
Upscale steakhouse in the Theater District. Known for prime dry-aged steaks and an extensive wine list. Elegant atmosphere perfect for pre-theater dining.
🍽️ Must Order: Dry-aged porterhouse, truffle mac and cheese
🌐 Website 📅 Reserve

Le Bernardin

⭐⭐⭐ 3 Michelin Stars $$$$$
📍 Address: 155 W 51st St
📏 Distance: 0.2 mi from August Wilson Theatre
⭐ Rating: 3 Michelin Stars, 4 NYT Stars
Eric Ripert's masterpiece. Three Michelin stars. Four stars from The New York Times. Consistently ranked among the best restaurants in the world. The seafood is flawless, the service is impeccable, the room is elegant but not stuffy. Ultimate splurge.
🍽️ Must Order: Tasting menu, any of the fish preparations
🌐 Website 📅 Reserve

Marea

⭐ Michelin Star Italian $$$$
📍 Address: 240 Central Park South
📏 Distance: 0.7 mi from August Wilson Theatre
⭐ Rating: 1 Michelin Star, James Beard Award
Michael White's flagship. Michelin-starred Italian on Central Park South. The room is stunning — Indonesian rosewood, airy, elegant. The focus is coastal Italian seafood: crudo, pasta, whole fish. This is where you go for a special occasion dinner with impeccable service.
🍽️ Must Order: Fusilli with red wine braised octopus, crudo selection
🌐 Website 📅 Reserve

Becco

Italian Theater Classic $$
📍 Address: 355 W 46th St (Restaurant Row)
📏 Distance: 0.3 mi from August Wilson Theatre
⭐ Rating: Lidia Bastianich's institution
Lidia Bastianich's Theater District institution. Fixed-price pasta tasting menu ($29.95), unlimited wine included. This is the theater crowd's go-to for reliable, affordable Italian before a show. Not Michelin-starred but a beloved NYC institution.
🍽️ Must Order: Sinfonia di pasta (unlimited pasta tasting), osso buco
🌐 Website 📅 Reserve

Toloache

$$ Mexican
📍 Address: 251 West 50th Street
📏 Distance: 0.12 mi from August Wilson Theatre
Upscale Mexican with excellent margaritas and guacamole. Lively but not too loud. Great for a fun pre-show meal. Modern Mexican cuisine in an elegant setting.
🍽️ Must Order: Tableside guacamole, margaritas, ceviche
🌐 Website 📅 Reserve

Beast & Butterflies

Rooftop $$
📍 Address: 226 W 52nd St (M Social Hotel)
📏 Distance: 0.01 mi from August Wilson Theatre
Literally next door to the theater. Indoor-outdoor rooftop bar with wrap-around terrace offering views of Times Square. This is your pre-show cocktail spot — close enough that you can sip a drink with a view and still make your 7:30 curtain.
🍽️ Must Order: Craft cocktails, Times Square views
🌐 Website 📅 Reserve

The Rickey Cocktail Lounge

Rooftop $$
📍 Address: 210 W 55th St (Dream Midtown)
📏 Distance: 0.13 mi from August Wilson Theatre
Upscale rooftop lounge with craft cocktails and city views. More of a "scene" than Beast & Butterflies, but great for a nightcap after the show. Stylish setting with panoramic Manhattan views.
🍽️ Must Order: Craft cocktails, skyline views
🌐 Website 📅 Reserve

Bar Centrale

NYC Institution $$$
📍 Address: 324 W 46th St (above Tony's di Napoli)
📏 Distance: 0.4 mi from August Wilson Theatre
Hidden speakeasy above Tony's di Napoli. Theater industry hangout — you'll see actors, producers, and critics here after shows. Excellent martinis, old-school NYC vibe. This is where the Broadway crowd goes.
🍽️ Must Order: Classic martini, post-show atmosphere
🌐 Website

Russian Samovar & Tolstoy's Lounge

NYC Institution $$$
📍 Address: 256 W 52nd Street
📏 Distance: 0.06 mi from August Wilson Theatre
Authentic Russian restaurant with vodka infusions (40+ house-infused vodkas), caviar, and live piano. Old-world charm, open late. Mikhail Baryshnikov is a regular. Perfect for late-night post-show drinks.
🍽️ Must Order: Vodka flights, caviar, live piano
🌐 Website 📅 Reserve
