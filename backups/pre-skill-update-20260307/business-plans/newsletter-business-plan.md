## The Model

We launch 1-3 hyper-niche newsletters, grow them to 5,000-10,000 subscribers each, and monetize through sponsorships + premium subscriptions. Fully AI-automated curation with Geoff's final editorial oversight.

**Revenue Streams:**
- **Sponsorships:** $500-2,000 per issue (at 5K+ subscribers)
- **Premium subscriptions:** $10-20/month
- **Affiliate revenue:** 10-30% of sales

**Math to $5K:**
- 5,000 subscribers
- 2 sponsors/month at $1,000 each = $2,000
- 200 premium subscribers at $15 = $3,000
- **Total: $5,000/month**

## The First 10 Steps (Detailed)

### Step 1: Pick Your Niche (Geoff — 1 hour)

**Evaluation Criteria:**
- Audience has money (B2B or affluent consumers)
- Content can be curated (news, trends, insights)
- Geoff has interest/expertise
- Existing newsletters prove demand
- Clear sponsor potential

**Top Recommendation: Healthcare Tech Weekly**
- **Audience:** Health IT professionals, digital health founders
- **Why it works:** Geoff's network, high-value audience, sponsor demand
- **Competition:** Moderate (can differentiate with Geoff's insider perspective)
- **Monetization:** Health tech vendors, consulting firms, SaaS companies

### Step 2: Validate the Niche (Me — 3 hours)

**Research:**
- Search "[niche] newsletter" — how many exist?
- Check Substack for similar newsletters
- Reddit: Size of r/[niche] community
- LinkedIn: How many professionals in this space?
- Potential sponsors: Who's already advertising?

**Validation Checklist:**
- [ ] 3+ existing newsletters with 5K+ subscribers
- [ ] 10+ potential sponsors identified
- [ ] Active Reddit community (10K+ members)
- [ ] Clear monetization path

### Step 3: Define the Angle (Geoff + Me — 1 hour)

**Healthcare Tech Weekly — Unique Angle:**
"The insider's briefing on health tech — what the VCs are funding, what the hospitals are buying, and what it means for your career. Curated by a health tech CPO."

**Differentiation:**
- Geoff's real industry experience
- Focus on "so what" analysis, not just news
- Exclusive insights from Geoff's network
- Concise: 5-minute read max

### Step 4: Set Up Infrastructure (Me — 4 hours)

**Beehiiv Setup:**
- Custom domain (healthcaretechweekly.com)
- Newsletter template design
- Welcome email sequence
- Referral program setup
- Premium tier configuration

**Social Media:**
- Twitter/X account
- LinkedIn page
- Posting schedule: 2x/day

### Step 5: Build Content System (Me — 6 hours)

**The Daily Workflow:**
```
6:00 AM PT (Me)
├── Scrape 50+ sources (RSS, Twitter, news)
├── AI summarizes top 10 stories
├── Geoff reviews and picks top 5
├── I draft newsletter
├── Geoff final edit (15 min)
└── Schedule for 8:00 AM PT
```

**Sources to Monitor:**
- Healthcare IT News
- HIMSS
- Rock Health funding reports
- TechCrunch health section
- LinkedIn thought leaders
- Twitter health tech accounts
- Company press releases

**Newsletter Format:**
```
Subject: [Healthcare Tech Weekly] + [Headline Story]

Good morning,

Here's what matters in health tech this week:

🔥 [Story 1]: [One-line summary]
   → [Why it matters - 2 sentences]

📈 [Story 2]: [One-line summary]
   → [Why it matters - 2 sentences]

💰 [Story 3 - Funding]: [Company raised $X]
   → [What they do + investor significance]

🏥 [Story 4 - Hospital]: [Implementation news]
   → [Industry implications]

🤖 [Story 5 - AI/Tech]: [New tech announcement]
   → [Practical impact]

---

💡 Geoff's Take: [2-3 sentences of original analysis]

See you next week,
Geoff

P.S. [Engagement question or CTA]
```
