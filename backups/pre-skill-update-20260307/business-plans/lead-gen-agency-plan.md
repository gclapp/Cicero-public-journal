## The First 10 Steps (Detailed)

### Step 1: Pick Your Niche (Geoff — 30 min)
**Options (non-fertility healthcare OK):**
- Healthcare IT companies (selling to hospitals)
- Medical device startups (B2B sales)
- Legal tech companies
- Marketing agencies (B2B focused)
- SaaS companies (specific vertical)
- Cybersecurity firms

**Criteria:**
- High contract values ($10K+ ACV)
- Clear decision-maker (VP Sales, CEO)
- Active hiring (signal of growth)
- Geoff has some network/understanding

### Step 2: Set Up Infrastructure (Me — 4 hours)
- Apollo.io Professional ($99/mo)
- Clay Starter ($149/mo)
- Instantly.ai ($37/mo)
- Buy 3 domains (~$50)
- Set up Google Workspace for domains

### Step 3: Build Lead Gen System (Me — 6 hours)
**Apollo Setup:**
- Build search filters for ICP
- Create saved lists
- Set up enrichment fields

**Clay Workflow:**
- Import Apollo leads
- Enrich: company size, tech stack, recent news
- AI personalization: custom opening lines
- Export to Instantly

### Step 4: Create Email Sequences (Me — 3 hours)
**Sequence 1 (Value-First):**
```
Subject: {{company}} + {{pain_point}}

Hi {{first_name}},

I was researching {{company}} and noticed {{personalized_observation}}.

{{relevant_case_study_or_insight}}

Worth a conversation?

Best,
Geoff
```

**Sequence 2 (Problem-Agitation):**
```
Subject: {{first_name}}, quick question

Hi {{first_name}},

Most {{industry}} companies I speak with struggle with {{common_problem}}.

{{specific_example}}

Is this on your radar?

Geoff
```

### Step 5: Warm Up Domains (Me — ongoing)
- Send 5-10 emails/day per domain
- Gradually ramp to 50/day over 2 weeks
- Monitor deliverability

### Step 6: Launch Test Campaign (Me — 2 hours)
- 50 leads per niche
- 3 niches = 150 total leads
- Track: open rates, reply rates, meeting bookings

### Step 7: Handle Responses (Geoff — 30 min/day)
- Reply to interested prospects
- Book discovery calls
- Track in HubSpot CRM

### Step 8: Run Sales Calls (Geoff — 45 min each)
**Discovery Call Script:**
1. Current lead gen process?
2. What's working/not working?
3. Cost per lead currently?
4. Ideal lead profile?
5. Decision timeline?

**Proposal:**
- "We'll deliver 20 qualified leads/month for $1,500"
- "First month guarantee: if we don't hit 20, you don't pay"

### Step 9: Deliver First Leads (Me — ongoing)
- Research each lead manually (first month)
- Include: contact info, company context, personalization
- Format: Google Sheet or CSV
- Deliver weekly

### Step 10: Optimize & Scale (Me — ongoing)
- Track lead quality feedback
- Refine targeting
- Automate more of the process
- Upsell to Growth tier
