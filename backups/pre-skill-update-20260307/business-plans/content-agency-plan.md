## The Model

We create fully automated content for SMBs who need consistent output but can't hire full-time writers. Blog posts, social media, email newsletters — all AI-powered, human-edited.

**Pricing Tiers:**
- **Basic:** $499/month (4 blog posts + 16 social posts)
- **Pro:** $999/month (8 blog posts + 32 social posts + 1 newsletter)
- **Agency:** $2,499/month (unlimited content, 2 brands)

**Math to $5K:** 5 Pro clients = $5,000 MRR

## The First 10 Steps (Detailed)

### Step 1: Pick Your Niche (Geoff — 30 min)
**Best options for Geoff:**
1. **Healthcare IT** — Network advantage, high-value clients
2. **B2B Professional Services** — Lawyers, accountants, consultants
3. **Local LA/SF Businesses** — Geographic advantage

**Criteria:**
- Content-hungry (need regular blog/social)
- $5K+ monthly marketing budgets
- Decision-maker is accessible
- Geoff has some connection/understanding

### Step 2: Build Content System (Me — 8 hours)

**The Workflow:**
```
1. Keyword Research (Ahrefs)
   ↓
2. Content Brief Creation (AI)
   ↓
3. First Draft (Claude/GPT)
   ↓
4. Human Edit (VA or Me)
   ↓
5. Client Review (Google Docs)
   ↓
6. Final Polish (Me)
   ↓
7. Publish/Deliver (Buffer or raw files)
```

**Automation with Make.com:**
- Trigger: New client onboarded
- Action: Create content calendar in Notion
- Action: Schedule keyword research
- Action: Generate content briefs
- Action: Assign to writer (Claude + VA)

### Step 3: Create Templates (Me — 4 hours)

**Blog Post Template:**
```
Title: [Keyword-Optimized Title]
Word Count: 1,200-1,500
Structure:
- Hook (problem/agitation)
- Solution overview
- 3-5 key points with examples
- Actionable takeaways
- CTA

Tone: Professional but conversational
SEO: Primary keyword in H1, 2-3 in body
```

**Social Post Templates:**
- Educational carousel (5-7 slides)
- Thought leadership text post
- Behind-the-scenes story
- Client testimonial spotlight
- Industry news commentary

### Step 4: Build Portfolio (Me — 6 hours)

Create 3 complete sample packages:
1. **Blog Post** (1,500 words, SEO-optimized)
2. **Social Posts** (8 posts for the same topic)
3. **Email Newsletter** (1 newsletter)

**Topics (Healthcare IT example):**
- "How AI is Reducing Hospital Readmission Rates"
- "5 EHR Integration Challenges (And How to Solve Them)"
- "The Future of Telehealth: 2025 Predictions"

### Step 5: Set Up Website (Me — 3 hours)

**Carrd Website Sections:**
- Hero: "Content That Converts — Fully Automated"
- Problem: "You know content matters, but you don't have time"
- Solution: "AI-powered content, human-edited quality"
- Portfolio: 3 sample pieces
- Pricing: 3 tiers
- FAQ: Common objections
- CTA: "Book a Free Content Audit"

### Step 6: Build Outreach List (Me — 4 hours)

**Sources:**
- LinkedIn Sales Navigator (filter by industry, company size)
- Apollo.io (B2B database)
- Local business directories
- Geoff's existing network

**Target Profile:**
- 10-100 employees
- Has a blog (but inconsistent)
- Active on social media (but sporadic)
- Marketing manager or CEO as decision-maker

### Step 7: Create Outreach Sequence (Me — 2 hours)

**LinkedIn Connection + Email Combo:**

**LinkedIn Message 1:**
```
Hi [Name], 

I came across [Company] and noticed your blog has great insights on [topic]. 

Quick question: Are you currently publishing consistently, or is content always on the "we should do more of that" list?

Geoff
```

**Email Follow-up (3 days later):**
```
Subject: [Company]'s content opportunity

Hi [Name],

I help [industry] companies publish consistent, high-quality content without hiring a full-time writer.

I created a sample blog post for [Company] — "[Sample Title]" — would you like me to send it over? No obligation, just thought it might be useful.

Best,
Geoff
```

### Step 8: Launch Outreach (Me — ongoing)

**Daily Targets:**
- 20 LinkedIn connections
- 10 personalized emails
- 3 follow-ups to previous contacts

**Metrics to Track:**
- Connection acceptance rate (target: 30%+)
- Email open rate (target: 40%+)
- Reply rate (target: 10%+)
- Meeting booking rate (target: 5%+)

### Step 9: Run Sales Calls (Geoff — 30 min each)

**Discovery Questions:**
1. Current content process?
2. What's your publishing frequency goal?
3. Who's writing content now?
4. What's the biggest bottleneck?
5. What would consistent content mean for your business?

**The Pitch:**
"We create 8 blog posts and 32 social posts per month for $999. Everything is researched, written, edited, and delivered ready to publish. You review and approve — we handle the rest."

**Objection Handling:**
- "Quality concerns?" → Show portfolio, offer first month guarantee
- "Too expensive?" → Compare to hiring a writer ($4K+/month)
- "AI-generated?" → Emphasize human editing, research, strategy

### Step 10: Onboard & Deliver (Me + VA — ongoing)

**Onboarding Process:**
1. Content strategy call (30 min — Geoff)
2. Brand voice guide creation (Me)
3. First month content calendar (Me)
4. Weekly delivery schedule (Me)
5. Monthly review call (30 min — Geoff)

**Quality Control:**
- Every piece reviewed by VA
- Geoff spot-checks 20%
- Client feedback incorporated immediately
