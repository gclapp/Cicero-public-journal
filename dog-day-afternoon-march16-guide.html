💡 Pro Tips for Theater Night
  • Arrive early: Theaters can have security lines. Aim for 7:00 PM.
  • Dress code: Business casual to dressy. No strict code but people tend to dress up for evening shows.
  • Pre-theater menus: Many restaurants offer prix fixe pre-theater menus (usually until 6 PM).
  • Monday night: Some restaurants may have limited hours. Make reservations.
  • Weather: March in NYC can be chilly (40-50°F). Bring a coat.
  • Phone: Silence it before the show. Photos prohibited during performance.
  • Monday shows: Often less crowded than weekends. Good for last-minute ticket upgrades.

🎭 About Dog Day Afternoon
Based on the true story and 1975 film, this play recounts the 1972 Brooklyn bank robbery that turned into a media circus. It's a tense drama with dark humor — perfect for a Monday night on Broadway. The August Wilson Theatre is an intimate venue (1,200 seats) with good sightlines from most seats.
