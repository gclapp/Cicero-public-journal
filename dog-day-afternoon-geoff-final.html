🥩 GEOFF'S TOP PICKS — Steakhouses & NYC Institutions

Keens Steakhouse #1 GEOFF PICK NYC INSTITUTION STEAKHOUSE
$$$$ | 72 W 36th St (Garment District) | 0.9 mi from theater
Established 1885. This is THE classic New York steakhouse. Famous for the 90,000 clay pipes hanging from the ceiling (including pipes belonging to Teddy Roosevelt, JFK, and Babe Ruth). The mutton chop is legendary — it's their signature. Old-school, dark wood, historic, unapologetically masculine. This is where you go for a quintessential NYC steakhouse experience.

Perfect for: Pre-theater dinner (5:00 PM reservation recommended)
Must-order: Mutton chop, dry-aged steaks, scotch selection

Gallaghers Steakhouse CLOSEST STEAKHOUSE NYC CLASSIC STEAKHOUSE
$$$$ | 228 West 52nd Street | 0.02 mi from theater
Since 1927. Right next door to the theater — you literally can't get closer. The iconic neon sign is a NYC landmark. Dry-aged steaks in a window display, old-school atmosphere, red leather banquettes. This is where theater-goers have dined for nearly a century.

Perfect for: Convenient pre-theater dinner (5:30 PM)
Must-order: Dry-aged ribeye, classic martini
