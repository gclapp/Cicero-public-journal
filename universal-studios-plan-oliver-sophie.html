💡 Pro Tips for Success

1. Download the App
Real-time wait times, show schedules, mobile food ordering. Essential.
2. Single Rider Lines
If kids are OK riding separately, single rider lines are much faster for Mario Kart and Transformers.
3. Child Swap (Rider Switch)
If Sophie can't ride something Oliver can, ask for "Child Swap" at the entrance. One parent rides while other waits with Sophie, then you swap without re-waiting.
4. SUPER NINTENDO WORLD Strategy
• Get there at rope drop (park opening)
• Mario Kart first — lines get brutal by 10 AM
• Power-Up Bands are fun but not required ($40 each)
• Toadstool Cafe requires reservation via app
5. Pack Smart
• Sunscreen & hats — limited shade
• Portable phone charger — you'll use the app constantly
• Snacks — food is expensive ($15-20 per meal)
• Light jacket — mornings can be cool
6. Know When to Quit
Kids will hit a wall around 4-5 PM. Don't push it. Better to leave on a high note than meltdown mode.

🎯 What to Consider at Universal Studios Hollywood

From your email, here's what to consider:

1. Height Requirements Are Everything
Sophie at 6 may not hit 40" for Mario Kart, Transformers, etc. Have a plan B (shows, play areas) so she doesn't feel left out. Use Child Swap for rides Oliver can do but Sophie can't.

2. SUPER NINTENDO World is the Draw
This is why you're going. But it's small and gets PACKED. Rope drop is essential. If Mario Kart has 90+ min wait later, skip it — not worth it with antsy kids.

3. Skip the Studio Tour (Good Call)
60 minutes is an eternity for kids. They'll get bored. The rides and shows are more engaging.

4. WaterWorld vs. Kung Fu Panda
Both are 20 min shows. Kung Fu Panda is 4D, seated, air-conditioned. WaterWorld has explosions and stunts but can be scary/loud. Do Kung Fu Panda if you only have time for one.

5. Jurassic World Water Ride
You'll get soaked. Bring ponchos or accept you'll be wet. Sophie may not meet 42" height. Consider doing this last if you do it at all.

6. Lower Lot is a Commitment
Escalators down are long. Once you're down there, you want to do everything before coming back up. Plan accordingly.

7. Character Meet & Greets
Mario, Luigi, Minions, Transformers — these can be highlights and don't have height requirements. Check app for times.

8. Know When to Call It
6-7 hours is plenty. Don't try to do everything. Better to leave wanting more than leave exhausted and cranky.
