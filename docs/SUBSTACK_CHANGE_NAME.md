# Substack: How to Change Publication Name

## Step-by-Step

### Step 1: Go to Settings
1. Make sure you're on your dashboard
2. Look for **"Settings"** in one of these places:
   - Top navigation bar (next to your profile)
   - Left sidebar menu
   - Under your publication name
   - Gear icon ⚙️

### Step 2: Find Publication Details
In Settings, look for:
- **"Publication details"** or
- **"General"** or
- **"Basic info"** or
- **"Profile"**

### Step 3: Edit the Name
You should see fields for:
- **Publication name** ← Change this to "Human in the Loop"
- **Subdomain** ← Change this to "humanintheloop"
- **Description** ← Add your description

### Step 4: Save
Click **"Save changes"** or **"Update publication"**

## If You Can't Find Settings

**Try these URLs directly:**

1. https://substack.com/settings
2. https://substack.com/publish/settings
3. https://substack.com/dashboard/settings

**Or:**

1. Go to your publication URL (geoffclapp.substack.com)
2. Click your profile picture
3. Look for "Settings" or "Edit publication"
