## Best Days/Times (Based on B2B Engagement Data)

**LinkedIn:**
- Best days: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
- Best times: 8-10 AM PT, 12-1 PM PT
- Avoid: Friday afternoons, weekends

**Twitter/X:**
- Best days: Tuesday-Thursday
- Best times: 9-11 AM PT, 1-3 PM PT
- More forgiving for off-hours posts

**Substack:**
- Best day: Wednesday morning
- Consistency matters more than exact timing

## Engagement Strategy

**Daily (15-20 min):**
- Reply to all comments on your posts
- Comment on 3-5 posts from others in AI/healthcare space
- Respond to DMs within 4 hours

**Weekly (1 hour):**
- Deep engagement with 2-3 thought leaders' content
- Follow new people who engaged with your posts
- Review analytics, adjust approach
