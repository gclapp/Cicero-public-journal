## My Top 5 Recommendations

### 1. **The Knowledge Navigator** ⭐
- Honors the 1987 inspiration
- Immediately communicates AI assistance
- Timeless, not tied to current tools
- Personal story built-in

### 2. **Navigator Notes**
- Softer than "The Knowledge Navigator"
- Implies ongoing exploration
- Personal journal feel
- Easy to say and remember

### 3. **Building with AI**
- Broad appeal (not just OpenClaw)
- Action-oriented
- Clear what readers will learn
- Future-proof as tools evolve

### 4. **My Digital Familiar**
- Unique, memorable
- Personal story (Cicero)
- Invites curiosity
- Warm, approachable

### 5. **Human in the Loop**
- AI industry term (keeps human involved)
- Emphasizes collaboration over replacement
- Technical but accessible
- Positions you as the human perspective
