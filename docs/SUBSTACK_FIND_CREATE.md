# Substack Dashboard - Finding Create Publication

## If There's No "Create Publication" Button

### Option 1: You're Already a Publication

Look for these instead:
- **"New post"** or **"Write"** button (top right)
- **"Settings"** link
- Your publication name at the top

If you see these, you already have a publication! Check the URL when you click around - it might be something like YOURNAME.substack.com

### Option 2: You Need to Switch to "Writer" Mode

In the dashboard, look for:
- **"Switch to writer"** toggle
- **"Writer dashboard"** link
- A mode selector (Reader vs Writer)

Click "Writer" or "Switch to writer"

### Option 3: Your Account is Reader-Only

If you only see:
- Subscriptions
- Reading list
- No writing tools

Then your account is set up as a reader. You need to:

1. Go to https://substack.com/signup?type=writer
2. Sign up with the same email
3. This converts your account to writer mode

### Option 4: Look in the Menu

Click your **profile picture** (top right) and look for:
- "Create publication"
- "Start a publication"  
- "New publication"
- "Add publication"
