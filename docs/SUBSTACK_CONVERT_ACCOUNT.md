# Substack: Convert Personal Account to Publication

## If You Already Have a Substack Account

### Step 1: Access Your Dashboard
1. Go to https://substack.com
2. Sign in with your existing account
3. Click your profile icon (top right)
4. Select "Dashboard"

### Step 2: Create a New Publication
1. In Dashboard, look for "Create publication" or "Start a publication"
2. Click it

### Step 3: Set Up "Human in the Loop"

**Publication Name:**
```
Human in the Loop
```

**Subdomain:**
```
humanintheloop
```
(This creates humanintheloop.substack.com)

**Description:**
```
Chief Product Officer at Progyny [PGNY]. Ex-Apple engineer. Lots of stuff in between. Still trying to build the Knowledge Navigator. Writing about AI in healthcare, OpenClaw, and human-AI collaboration - with working code you can steal and mistakes you can avoid.
```

### Step 4: Publication Settings

1. **Click "Create publication"**
2. You'll be taken to Settings

**In Settings, configure:**

**Publication Details:**
- Publication name: Human in the Loop
- Subdomain: humanintheloop
- Description: (as above)
- Publication icon: Upload Dr. Lobster logo

**Features:**
- ✅ Allow comments on posts
- ✅ Enable likes
- ✅ Show subscribe button
- Start with: Free (no paid subscriptions yet)

**About Page:**
Add this:
```
In machine learning, "human in the loop" refers to systems where humans participate in training, testing, or decision-making alongside AI.

I think it should mean more than that.

This publication is about building AI systems where humans aren't just participants—they're the point. Where technology amplifies judgment rather than replacing it. Where the compass (human direction) guides the code (AI capability).

I'm Geoff Clapp, Chief Product Officer at Progyny, and I've spent 30 years thinking about intelligent systems—from watching Apple's Knowledge Navigator video in 1987 to building my own AI assistant (Cicero) in 2026.

This is my journey of building in public. The tools, the mistakes, the lessons learned, and the systems that actually work.

Whether you're a product leader, engineer, or just AI-curious, I hope you find something useful here.

Let's build.
```

### Step 5: Save Settings
Click "Save" at the bottom.

### Step 6: Verify
Your publication should now be live at:
https://humanintheloop.substack.com

---

## Troubleshooting

**If subdomain is taken:**
- Try: humanintheloop-ai
- Or: humanintheloop-blog
- Or: hitl (acronym)

**If you can't find "Create publication":**
- Some accounts are already publications
- Check if you're in "Reader" mode vs "Writer" mode
- Look for "Switch to writer" option
