# Lose It! Daily Email to Cicero

## What This Does
- Lose It! emails you a daily summary
- You forward it to [REDACTED]
- Cicero extracts: calories, protein, carbs, fat, water
- Appears in your morning check-in

---

## Step 1: Enable Daily Emails in Lose It!

1. Open **Lose It!** app
2. Tap **Profile** (bottom right)
3. Tap **Settings** (gear icon)
4. Scroll to **Notifications**
5. Enable **"Daily Summary Email"**
6. Set time: **9:00 PM** (or whenever you prefer)

---

## Step 2: Forward Email to Cicero

**When you receive the daily email:**

1. Open the email from Lose It!
2. Tap **Forward**
3. Send to: **[REDACTED]**
4. (Optional) Add note: "Lose It! daily summary"
5. Tap **Send**

---

## Option: Auto-Forward (Gmail)

**Set up automatic forwarding in Gmail:**

1. Open Gmail on computer
2. Click **Settings** (gear) → **See all settings**
3. Go to **Filters and Blocked Addresses**
4. Click **Create new filter**
5. In "From" field, enter: **noreply@loseit.com**
6. Click **Create filter**
7. Check **"Forward it to"**
8. Enter: **[REDACTED]**
9. Click **Create filter**

**Now all Lose It! emails auto-forward to me!**

## Troubleshooting

**Not receiving daily emails?**
- Check spam folder
- Verify email in Lose It! profile
- Try toggling notification off/on

**Data not showing in check-in?**
- Forward may take a few minutes
- I process emails hourly
- Check that email came from Lose It!
