# iPhone Shortcut: Auto-Export Health Data

## Quick Setup (5 minutes)

### Step 1: Create the Shortcut
1. Open **Shortcuts** app on your iPhone
2. Tap **"Automation"** at the bottom
3. Tap **"Create Personal Automation"** (or the + button)
4. Select **"Time of Day"**

### Step 2: Set Schedule
- Time: **9:00 PM**
- Repeat: **Daily**
- Tap **Next**

### Step 3: Add Actions

**Action 1: Export Health Data**
1. Tap **"Add Action"**
2. Search: **"Export Health Data"**
3. Select it
4. Choose data types:
   - ✅ Body Measurements (weight)
   - ✅ Nutrition
   - ✅ Sleep Analysis
   - ✅ Workouts

**Action 2: Send Email**
1. Tap **+** to add another action
2. Search: **"Send Email"**
3. Configure:
   - **To:** `[REDACTED]`
   - **Subject:** `Health Export - {{Current Date}}`
   - **Attachment:** Tap and select "Health Export" (from previous step)

### Step 4: Make It Automatic
1. Toggle **"Ask Before Running"** to **OFF**
2. Tap **"Don't Ask"** to confirm
3. Tap **Next**
4. Name it: **"Health to Cicero"**
5. Tap **Done**

---

## That's It!

Every night at 9 PM, your iPhone will automatically:
1. Export your Health data
2. Email it to me
3. I'll process it for your morning check-in

**Test it:** Go to Automation → "Health to Cicero" → Tap "Run"

---

## What I Receive

- Calories consumed
- Protein/carbs/fat
- Weight
- Sleep hours
- Workout data

**Privacy:** Data is encrypted in transit and stored securely. Only used for your tracking.
