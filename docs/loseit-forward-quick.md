## Option B: Auto-Forward (Set up once)

### Step 1: Enable Daily Emails in Lose It!
1. Open **Lose It!** app
2. Tap **Profile** → **Settings** (gear)
3. **Notifications** → Enable **"Daily Summary Email"**
4. Set time: **9:00 PM**

### Step 2: Set Up Gmail Auto-Forward

**On your computer:**
1. Go to **Gmail** → **Settings** (gear) → **See all settings**
2. Click **"Filters and Blocked Addresses"**
3. Click **"Create new filter"**
4. In "From" field, enter: `noreply@loseit.com`
5. Click **"Create filter"**
6. Check **"Forward it to"**
7. Enter: `[REDACTED]`
8. Click **"Create filter"**

**Done!** All Lose It! emails now auto-forward to me.
