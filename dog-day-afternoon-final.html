🎬 About the Show

Dog Day Afternoon is based on the true story of a Brooklyn bank robbery gone wrong in 1972. What was supposed to be a quick heist turned into a 14-hour hostage situation that played out live on television, becoming one of the first major media spectacles of the modern era.

The play explores:

  • The desperation behind the crime
  • The relationship between the robber and his hostages
  • The media circus that surrounded the event
  • The human connections formed under extreme pressure

Runtime: Approximately 2 hours 30 minutes (with intermission)
Recommended for: Ages 16+ (strong language, intense situations)

🍸 Top 3 Post-Show Bar Options

🥇 #1 Bar Centrale TOP PICK

📍 324 W 46th St (above Tony's di Napoli) | 🚶 5 min walk from theater

Hidden speakeasy, theater industry hangout. You'll see actors, producers, and critics here. Excellent martinis, old-school NYC vibe. This is where the Broadway crowd goes after shows.

Best for: Classic martinis, theater atmosphere, celebrity spotting

🥈 #2 Russian Samovar & Tolstoy's Lounge

📍 256 W 52nd Street | 🚶 1 min walk from theater

Authentic Russian restaurant with 40+ house-infused vodkas, caviar, and live piano. Old-world charm, open until 2 AM. Mikhail Baryshnikov is a regular. Perfect for late-night drinks.

Best for: Vodka flights, caviar, live piano, late night

🥉 #3 The Rickey Cocktail Lounge at Dream Midtown

📍 210 W 55th St | 🚶 5 min walk OR 🚕 3 min taxi

Upscale rooftop lounge with craft cocktails and city views. More of a "scene" than the others, stylish setting with panoramic Manhattan views. Great for a nightcap with a view.

Best for: Rooftop views, craft cocktails, stylish atmosphere
