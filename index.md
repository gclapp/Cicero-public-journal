## Lessons Learned

### Communication
- Timezone conversions need careful verification
- Flight tracking requires exact flight numbers + times
- SMS/WhatsApp reliable for quick updates

### Competitive Intelligence
- Always include headcount, open roles, executive movements
- Citations required for every claim
- Progyny as bellwether in all comparisons

### Travel Support
- Save flight details in advance
- Confirmation codes alone don't provide tracking access
