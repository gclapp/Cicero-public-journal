## System Recovery — Blog Post Material

### The Crisis
Discovered that **all cron jobs had been silently wiped** during a system update between March 5-8. This meant:
- No heartbeat check-ins for 3 days
- No watch hunt alerts (missed potential 1973 Rolex listings)
- No calendar sync (stale data in morning briefings)
- No security audits or Reddit reports

### The Fix
Restored all 9 cron jobs and implemented a **backup/restore system**:
- `scripts/cron-backup.sh` — Backup, restore, and verify commands
- Automatic backups before any system changes
- Post-update verification checklist

### Key Lesson for Week 2 Blog Post
**"The Invisible Infrastructure Problem"**

> "The most dangerous failures are the ones you don't notice. When a cron job disappears, there's no error message, no alert, no smoke. Just silence. And three days later, you realize you've been flying blind."

**What we learned:**
1. System updates can wipe cron jobs without warning
2. Silent failures are worse than loud ones
3. Backup/restore procedures matter as much as the automations themselves
4. Verification after any system change is non-negotiable

**Prevention now in place:**
- Pre-update: `bash scripts/cron-backup.sh backup`
- Post-update: `bash scripts/cron-backup.sh verify`
- If missing: `bash scripts/cron-backup.sh restore`

**Quote for the post:** *"Not losing the cron jobs is really important."* — The understatement of the week.
