## Proactive Restaurant-Friend Matching System

**When Geoff mentions a restaurant (visited or planning to visit), I will:**

1. **Ask:** "Who else of your friends might like this place?"
2. **Capture:** Their name + why they'd like it (interests, location, vibe)
3. **Track:** Add to the database with match reasoning
4. **Suggest:** In the future, recommend this restaurant when relevant

### Example Flow:

**Geoff:** "Went to [New Restaurant] tonight..."
**Me:** "How was it? Also — who else of your friends might like this spot?"
**Geoff:** "Adam would love it — casual, great for groups, near the beach"
**Me:** *Adds to database: "[New Restaurant] → Good for: Adam Dole (Malibu surfer, likes casual group spots)"*

**Later:**
**Me:** "You haven't seen Adam in 3 weeks — want to check out [New Restaurant]? It's casual, group-friendly, perfect for him."

### Matching Criteria I Track:

**For each restaurant, I capture:**
- **Vibe:** Casual/fancy, loud/quiet, date night/group
- **Location:** Neighborhood, proximity to friends
- **Cuisine:** Type of food, specific preferences
- **Price:** $-$$$$ 
- **Best for:** Specific friend names + why

**For each friend, I track:**
- **Location:** Where they live (Malibu, Westside, etc.)
- **Interests:** Surfing, fancy dining, casual hangs, etc.
- **Preferences:** Cuisine likes, vibe preferences
- **Social style:** Group dinners vs. 1-on-1, casual vs. formal

### Active Questions I'll Ask:

When you mention restaurants, I'll ask:
- "Who else would like this place?"
- "Is this more of a date spot or group spot?"
- "What type of friend is this perfect for?"
- "Casual like American Beauty, or fancier?"
