# Weekly Review

Use this brief to close the week and carry forward the right work.

## Purpose

Answer: what happened, what was decided, what changed, what remains open, and what should shape next week?

## Primary Inputs

- Full work calendar for the week
- Meeting notes and processed summaries
- Completed and open action notes
- Decision notes
- Project notes
- Competitive intelligence, when relevant
- Sunday and Wednesday briefs from the same week

## Output Shape

- Week summary
- Key decisions
- Major progress
- Open loops
- Risks
- People and relationships to follow up with
- Carry-forward list for next week
- Long-term memory candidates

## Storage

Generated weekly reviews should live in this folder with a date prefix:

```text
YYYY-MM-DD - Weekly Review.md
```
