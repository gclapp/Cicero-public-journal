# Sunday Morning - Week Ahead

Use this brief to prepare the coming week before it starts.

## Purpose

Answer: what is coming, what matters, what should Geoff prepare, and what should get closed early?

## Primary Inputs

- Work calendar for the upcoming Monday-Sunday window
- Open PGNY action notes
- Recent PGNY decisions
- Important unfinished threads
- Travel or schedule constraints
- Upcoming executive, board, customer, partner, and product strategy meetings
- Relevant context from `shared-inbox/Inbox/`

## Output Shape

- Week headline
- Calendar shape
- High-leverage meetings
- Preparation needed
- Decisions likely to come up
- Risks and watch items
- Follow-ups to close early
- Suggested focus for the week

## Storage

Generated Sunday briefs should live in this folder with a date prefix:

```text
YYYY-MM-DD - Sunday Morning - Week Ahead.md
```
