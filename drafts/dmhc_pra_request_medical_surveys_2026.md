## REQUEST FOR RECORDS

Pursuant to the California Public Records Act, Government Code Section 6250 et seq., I hereby request access to and copies of the following public records:

### RECORDS REQUESTED

**All medical survey reports** (including but not limited to routine surveys, focused surveys, and follow-up surveys) conducted by or on behalf of the Department of Managed Health Care for the following health plans, covering the period from January 1, 2020 through May 30, 2026:

1. **Blue Cross of California** (including all affiliated entities)
2. **Blue Shield of California** (including all affiliated entities)
3. **Kaiser Foundation Health Plan, Inc.** (Kaiser Permanente)
4. **Health Net, Inc.** (including all affiliated entities)
5. **Anthem Blue Cross** (including all affiliated entities)
6. **Aetna Health of California, Inc.** (including all affiliated entities)
7. **L.A. Care Health Plan**

### SCOPE OF REQUEST

The requested records include, but are not limited to:

- **Routine medical survey reports** (full, comprehensive surveys)
- **Focused medical survey reports** (targeted surveys on specific issues)
- **Follow-up medical survey reports** (surveys conducted to verify correction of deficiencies)
- **Survey findings and determinations**
- **Deficiency citations and corrective action plans**
- **Survey team reports and worksheets**
- **Any related correspondence, attachments, or appendices**

### FORMAT REQUESTED

I request that these records be provided in **electronic format (PDF)** to the extent they exist in that format, in accordance with Government Code Section 6253.9. Electronic format is requested for cost efficiency and to facilitate review and analysis of the records.

### FEE WAIVER REQUEST

I request a waiver of fees pursuant to Government Code Section 6253(b) and 6253.1. These records are of significant public interest as they relate to:

1. The oversight and regulation of health care service plans that serve millions of Californians
2. Consumer protection and the public's right to know about the quality of care provided by regulated health plans
3. Governmental accountability and transparency in the regulation of health care entities

The disclosure of these records will serve the public interest by promoting transparency in health plan oversight and informing the public about the quality and compliance of major health plans operating in California.
