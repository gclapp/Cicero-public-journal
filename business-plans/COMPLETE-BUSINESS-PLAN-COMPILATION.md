## RECOMMENDED: PORTFOLIO APPROACH

**Test all 3 simultaneously for 60 days, then double down on winners.**

1. Diversification — not dependent on single model
2. Learning — see what actually works
3. Optionality — pivot quickly based on data
4. Revenue stacking — multiple streams compound
5. Risk reduction — if one fails, others may succeed

## KILL CRITERIA (Be Ruthless)

After 60 days, we evaluate:

**Lead Gen:**
- ✅ Continue: 1+ client at $1,500+/mo, 10%+ reply rates
- ❌ Kill: No clients, <5% reply rate after 200 leads

**Newsletter:**
- ✅ Continue: 500+ subscribers, 45%+ open rate
- ❌ Kill: <300 subscribers, <35% open rate

**Content Agency:**
- ✅ Continue: 1+ pilot client, positive feedback
- ❌ Kill: No clients, negative feedback on samples

**Important:** Killing one isn't failure — it's data. We double down on winners.
