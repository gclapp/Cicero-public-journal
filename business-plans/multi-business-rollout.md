# Multi-Business Rollout Plan

**Philosophy:** Start lean, validate fast, double down on what works  
**Geoff's time:** 2 hours/day total (split across initiatives)  
**My time:** Full capacity across all three  
**Investment:** $4,500 first 3 months  
**Goal:** Have 2-3 revenue streams by month 6  

---

## The Portfolio Approach

Instead of going all-in on one, we run **three parallel experiments** for 60-90 days, then double down on the winner(s).

| Business | Month 1-2 | Month 3-4 | Month 5-6 |
|----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| **Lead Gen Agency** | Setup + first clients | Scale to $3K | Optimize or sunset |
| **Newsletter** | Setup + launch | Grow to 2K subs | Monetize or pivot |
| **Content Agency** | Validate demand | Pilot with 2 clients | Scale or kill |

**Content Agency:**
- ✅ **Continue if:** 1+ pilot client, positive feedback
- ❌ **Kill if:** No clients, negative feedback on samples

### Decision Point: End of Month 2

**Scenario A: All 3 showing promise**
- Continue all 3
- Scale investment
- Hire VA to help

**Scenario B: 2 working, 1 failing**
- Double down on 2 winners
- Kill the loser
- Reallocate time/budget

**Scenario C: 1 clear winner**
- Go all-in on winner
- Sunset others
- Maximize ROI

**Scenario D: All 3 struggling**
- Pivot to new niches
- Or reconsider model entirely
- (Unlikely given market demand)

## The "Portfolio Theory" Advantage

**Why 3 is better than 1:**

1. **Diversification:** Not dependent on single model
2. **Learning:** See what actually works vs. what should work
3. **Optionality:** Can pivot quickly based on data
4. **Revenue stacking:** Multiple streams compound faster
5. **Risk reduction:** If one fails, others may succeed

**Why not 5+?**
- Geoff's time gets too fragmented
- I can't execute well on too many fronts
- Better to do 3 things well than 5 things poorly
