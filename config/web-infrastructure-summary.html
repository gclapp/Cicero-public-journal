📈 Rate Limits & Best Practices

Current Limits

  • Brave Search API: 2,000 queries/month (free tier)
  • Scrapling Browser: ~4 pages/minute (to avoid detection)
  • Watch Hunt: 2x daily (9 AM & 6 PM PT)
  • Competitive Intel: 2x daily (7 AM & 2 PM PT)

Anti-Detection Measures

  • Random delays between requests (1-3 seconds)
  • Real browser headers and user agents
  • Session persistence with cookies
  • Headless browser with stealth plugins
