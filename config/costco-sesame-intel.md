# Competitive Intelligence Alert: Costco + Sesame + IVI RMA Partnership

**Date:** March 9, 2026 (announced)  
**Signal Level:** 🔴 HIGH — Major retail-fertility partnership

## The Deal

**Partners:**
- **Costco** (wholesale retail giant, 70M+ members)
- **Sesame** (direct-to-consumer healthcare marketplace)
- **IVI RMA** (fertility clinic network, part of RMA Global)

**What They're Offering:**
- Fertility care coordination and treatment access
- **Up to 80% savings on fertility medications** for Costco members
- Transparent pricing through Sesame's platform
- Access to IVI RMA's clinic network

## Why This Matters for Progyny

### 1. **New Competitive Dynamic**
- **Direct-to-employer (Progyny model)** vs **Direct-to-consumer via retail (Costco model)**
- Costco's 70M+ member base is massive — many are employed and may question why they need employer-sponsored fertility benefits if they can get discounted care through Costco

### 2. **Price Pressure**
- 80% medication savings is significant
- This puts pressure on the entire industry's pricing model
- Employers may start asking "Why can't we get 80% savings like Costco?"

### 3. **Channel Shift Risk**
- If employees start using Costco/Sesame instead of employer benefits, Progyny's utilization and revenue could be impacted
- This is a **consumer-driven disruption** to the B2B model

### 4. **Partnership Precedent**
- Retail + healthcare marketplace + clinic network is a new formula
- Could inspire copycats (Walmart, Amazon, Target)
- Sesame is now a player to watch in fertility specifically

## Key Quotes

> "In partnership with Costco and Sesame, leaders of the highest caliber, we are expanding access to quality fertility care and reducing barriers so more people across the country can build the families they desire." — IVI RMA
