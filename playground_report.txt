COMPLETE PLAYGROUND RESEARCH REPORT
For Kids Ages 6-10 Near Calabasas
Compiled: February 28, 2026

========================================
CLOSEST TO CALABASAS (Under 15 min)
========================================

1. BRANDON'S VILLAGE AT GATES CANYON PARK ⭐ HIGHEST RECOMMENDATION
   Location: Calabasas (in your backyard!)
   Size: 15,000 sq ft
   Renovated: 2024 ($1 million upgrade)
   Theme: "Above the Tree Line"
   Age Groups: Separate structures for 2-5 and 5-12
   Special Features: Fully inclusive design for all abilities
   Hours: Sunrise to 10 PM
   
   Why it's great: Brand new, massive, colorful, and designed for both younger 
   and older kids. This is your #1 starting point.

========================================
WITHIN 30 MINUTES
========================================

2. SERRANIA PARK
   Address: 20726 Wells Drive, Woodland Hills, CA 91364
   Drive Time: 15-20 minutes from Calabasas
   Phone: (818) 756-8189
   Hours: Dawn to Dusk, daily
   Playground: Renovated 2022
   Parking: Street parking + private lot
   
   Features: Excellent climbing structures perfect for ages 5-12, big slides, 
   large open space, hiking trails, dog park, running trails, bike parking.
   
   More info: 
   - Yelp: 140+ photos and reviews
   - Official: recreation.parks.lacity.gov/park/serrania

3. WOODLAND HILLS RECREATION CENTER (SHOUP PARK)
   Location: Woodland Hills
   Features: Multiple playgrounds in one area
   Extras: Sports fields, picnic areas
   
   Why go: Very popular with local families, lots of kids to play with, 
   well-maintained.

========================================
WORTH THE DRIVE (30-45 min) - DESTINATION PLAYGROUNDS
========================================

4. TONGVA PARK ⭐ EPIC DESTINATION
   Address: 1615 Ocean Ave, Santa Monica, CA 90401
   Drive Time: 35-40 minutes
   Size: 6 acres
   Style: Modern urban park
   
   Epic Features:
   - Three tall hillside slides built into the terrain
   - Climbing wall
   - Splash pad for cooling off
   - Art sculptures integrated into play areas
   - Walking distance to Santa Monica Pier
   - Beautifully landscaped with hills and ravines
   
   Make a day of it: Playground + lunch + Santa Monica Pier + beach
   Website: santamonica.gov/places/parks/tongva-park

5. SHANE'S INSPIRATION AT GRIFFITH PARK
   Location: Griffith Park (near LA Zoo)
   Drive Time: 35-40 minutes
   Size: Massive playground
   Type: All-abilities playground
   
   Features: One of LA's most famous playgrounds, tons of equipment for ages 5-12, 
   completely fenced and secure.
   
   Combo trip: Pair with LA Zoo or Travel Town (vintage trains) for a full day.

6. KIDSPACE CHILDREN'S MUSEUM - ARROYO ADVENTURE
   Location: Pasadena
   Drive Time: 35-40 minutes
   Cost: Admission required (~$15-20 per person)
   Website: kidspacemuseum.org
   
   Epic Outdoor Area:
   - Arroyo Adventure: Expansive multi-level environment
   - Stream wading area ("Splash & Flow")
   - Rock formations to climb
   - Treehouses and climbing structures
   - Physics park with interactive exhibits
   - Mimics natural landscape of local Arroyo Seco
   
   Best for: Full day adventure when you want more than just a playground.

========================================
INDOOR OPTIONS (For Hot/Rainy Days)
========================================

7. KIDS WORLD
   Location: Los Angeles (~40 min)
   Size: 20,000 sq ft
   Capacity: 800 people
   
   Features: State-of-the-art arcade games, laser tag arena, obstacle courses, 
   basketball court, Kids World Cafe & Grille.
   Website: kidsworldla.com

8. POSH & PETITE PLAYHOUSE
   Location: Woodland Hills (15 min)
   Type: Indoor play space
   
   Good for: Birthday parties, hot days, shorter play sessions.

MY TOP 3 RECOMMENDATIONS
========================================

1. START HERE: Brandon's Village
   Brand new (2024), massive, in Calabasas, perfect for Oliver & Sophie's ages. 
   Zero drive time.

2. DAY TRIP: Tongva Park
