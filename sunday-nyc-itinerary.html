🌅 Morning

10:00 AM
Brunch at The Smith (Midtown) BRUNCH

📍 956 2nd Avenue (at 51st St) | 🚶 10 min walk from The Westin

Why: Classic NYC brunch spot with excellent eggs Benedict, pancakes, and Bloody Marys. Lively atmosphere, perfect for starting your Sunday.

Must-order: Eggs Benedict, avocado toast, brunch cocktails

🌐 Website | 📅 Reserve

11:30 AM
Walk to Central Park PARK NYC CLASSIC

📍 Central Park (enter at 59th St & 5th Ave) | 🚶 15 min walk from The Smith

Why: The most iconic park in the world. Perfect for a romantic Sunday stroll, people watching, and classic NYC vibes.

Walk route: Enter at Grand Army Plaza → Bethesda Terrace & Fountain → The Mall → Bow Bridge

☀️ Afternoon

12:30 PM
Explore Central Park PARK

Highlights:

  • Bethesda Terrace: Iconic fountain and architecture
  • The Mall: Beautiful tree-lined promenade
  • Bow Bridge: Most photographed spot in the park
  • Strawberry Fields: John Lennon memorial

Tip: Grab coffee at the Bethesda Fountain cafe

2:00 PM
Walk to West Village via High Line NYC CLASSIC

Route: Central Park → Columbus Circle → Walk south through Hell's Kitchen → High Line entrance (30th St) → Walk the High Line south to West Village

Why: The High Line is a converted elevated railway turned park — unique NYC experience with art installations, gardens, and city views.

Duration: 45-60 min leisurely walk

3:30 PM
Explore West Village NYC CLASSIC

Why: Charming cobblestone streets, historic brownstones, boutique shops. One of the most romantic neighborhoods in NYC.

Walk around: Bleecker Street, Christopher Street, Washington Square Park (if time permits)

Shopping: Unique boutiques, bookstores, vintage shops

🌆 Evening

4:45 PM
Pre-Dinner Drinks at a West Village Bar

Option 1: Dante (52 Grove St) — Famous for Negronis, outdoor seating

Option 2: Employees Only (510 Hudson St) — Speakeasy-style cocktail bar

Option 3: Little Branch (20 7th Ave S) — Hidden basement speakeasy

5:00 PM
Dinner at L'Artusi CONFIRMED

📍 228 West 10th Street (Between Bleecker & Hudson)

Why: Modern Italian, intimate atmosphere, incredible pasta. One of NYC's best date night spots.

Must-order: Olive oil cake, any pasta, Italian wine

🌐 Website

7:00 PM
Post-Dinner Walk or Nightcap
