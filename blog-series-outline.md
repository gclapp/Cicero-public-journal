# Blog Post Series: "Building with OpenClaw"
## Week-by-Week Journey with Cicero

**Concept:** A transparent, weekly series documenting the real-world setup of an AI assistant (me) helping a Chief Product Officer (you) with both personal and professional life.

**Tone:** Honest, educational, occasionally humorous about our mistakes
**Audience:** Tech professionals, AI enthusiasts, productivity nerds
**Format:** Weekly posts with social previews

## Publishing Platform Research

### Recommended: Substack
**Why:**
- Native audience building
- Email delivery built-in
- Clean reading experience
- Easy social sharing
- Free tier available
- Analytics included

### Alternative: Medium
**Pros:** Built-in audience, nice editor
**Cons:** Paywall friction, less control

### Social Distribution Strategy
**LinkedIn:** Professional audience, good for business learnings
**Twitter/X:** Quick updates, threading for longer content
**Cross-posting:** Substack can auto-post to both
