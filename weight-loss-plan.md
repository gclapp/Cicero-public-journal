# 🎯 GEOFF'S 20-POUND WEIGHT LOSS PLAN
## Executive Lifestyle-Optimized | 10-12 Week Program

**Goal:** Lose 20 lbs safely and sustainably

## 📊 THE MATH (Evidence-Based)

- **1 lb fat = 3,500 calories**
- **20 lbs = 70,000 calorie deficit needed**
- **Safe rate:** 1.5-2 lbs/week = 750-1,000 calorie daily deficit
- **Your target:** 1,800-2,000 calories/day (depending on activity)
- **Protein target:** 150-180g/day (prevents muscle loss, keeps you full)

---

## 🍽️ NUTRITION STRATEGY

### Core Principles (No Fad Diets)

1. **High Protein (30-35% of calories)**
   - Keeps you full longer
   - Prevents muscle loss while losing fat
   - Higher thermic effect (burns more calories digesting)

2. **Lower Carbs (Not Zero - 20-25%)**
   - Focus on complex carbs: veggies, berries, beans
   - Limit: Bread, pasta, sugar, alcohol (especially on weekdays)

3. **Healthy Fats (40-45%)**
   - Avocado, nuts, olive oil, fish
   - Keeps hormones balanced

4. **No Calorie Counting Obsession**
   - Use MyFitnessPal for 2 weeks to learn portions
   - Then eyeball it using the "palm method"

### The "Geoff-Approved" Foods (Based on Your Preferences)

**✅ EAT FREELY:**
- **Proteins:** Steak, chicken, fish, eggs, Greek yogurt
- **Veggies:** Broccoli, spinach, salad greens, asparagus
- **Fats:** Avocado, olive oil, nuts, cheese (moderation)
- **Low-sugar fruits:** Berries, apples

**⚠️ LIMIT (Weekends only or small portions):**
- Pizza (your favorite - have 2 slices max, not 4)
- Burgers (bunless or half the bun)
- Thai/Chinese (rice on the side, focus on protein/veg)

**❌ MINIMIZE:**
- Alcohol (empty calories, lowers inhibition)
- Sugary drinks
- Late-night snacking

### Sample Day (Travel-Friendly)

**BREAKFAST (400-500 cal, 30g protein):**
- 3 eggs + 2 egg whites scrambled
- 1/2 avocado
- Black coffee

**LUNCH (500-600 cal, 40g protein):**
- Large salad with grilled chicken or steak
- Olive oil + vinegar dressing
- No bread/bun

**DINNER (600-700 cal, 50g protein):**
- 8oz steak or salmon
- Large portion of vegetables
- Small sweet potato OR no starch

**SNACKS (200-300 cal, 20g protein):**
- Greek yogurt + berries
- Handful of almonds
- Protein shake if needed

**TOTAL:** ~1,800 calories, ~140g protein
